# 外层重试函数语义实测(逐字取自 Dockerfile 的 retry())

## CASE A: 命令始终失败 → 3 次尝试后 FATAL + 退出码 1(fail-closed,不吞错)
[build] retry 1/3 after failure: false
[build] retry 2/3 after failure: false
[build] FATAL retry exhausted: false
CASE_A_EXIT=1

## CASE B: 前两次失败第三次成功 → 2 次重试后继续,退出码 0
flaky: fail #1
[build] retry 1/3 after failure: flaky
flaky: fail #2
[build] retry 2/3 after failure: flaky
flaky: ok on #3
continued after recovery
CASE_B_EXIT=0

## CASE C: 首次成功 → 不重试
no retry lines above = pass
CASE_C_EXIT=0
